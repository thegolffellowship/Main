How You Qualify — 14 Seats Per Chapter
  • 1 · Captain — your City NET Champion
  • 6 — final standings of The Fellowship Cup (top 6 from your chapter)
  • 1 — your City Match Play Champion
  • 4 — final standings of The Players Cup (top 4 from your chapter)
  • 2 — bonus spots for members of the former TGF chapters (DFW & Houston)
Seats & Alternates
Only bought-in players hold seats. Qualify in more than one contest and you take the seat where you placed higher — the seat you free up goes to the next player up from the alternates pool. Alternates are ranked by how high you finished relative to your field; ties go to whoever played more.
Rosters are decided after the City and TGF Championships — until then every seat is projected live from the current standings.
