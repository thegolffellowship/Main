Hi everyone,

This weekend at the Lone Star Cup we broke free from Golf Genius. Tonight is the first regular chapter night we run on our own system, in San Antonio and Austin. Your group keeps score on a phone, the leaderboard updates live as every group posts, and the results, payouts, points and handicaps all come straight from what your group enters.

How it works: one phone keeps score for the group, and one person keeps the paper card as usual. Everyone else can follow along on the leaderboard. Here's the step by step.

1. Open your group's scorecard

Tap "open your group's scorecard" in today's pairing email, or scan the QR code on your cart sign. The scorekeeper taps SCORE THIS GROUP. Everyone else can tap FOLLOW THE LEADERBOARD to watch the field live. Following changes nothing.

2. Tap your name

The first person to start keeps score for the group. Your playing handicap is already loaded from the starter sheet. Nothing to set up.

3. Enter each hole

Tap + or − for each player's gross score, then Save & Go to the next hole. If a player made par, you don't need to tap anything: every score starts at par, so just save. Gross scores only; the pops and net games are done for you. The maximum on any hole is a triple bogey. If the signal drops, keep going: every hole is saved on the phone and sends when the signal comes back.

4. Closest to the pin

On the closest-to-the-pin holes the phone asks who in your group is on the green. Pick the player, or No one on the green. No measuring needed: the next group sees who to beat, and the manager settles any disagreement.

5. Check the card against the paper card

After the last hole, read the phone's card against the paper card, hole by hole. Tap any number to fix it. When every hole matches, tap Card matches paper.

6. Photo of the paper card, then Submit

Take a photo of the paper card, pick who kept it, and tap Submit card.

7. Everyone signs their own card

The scorekeeper signs right on the scoring phone. Everyone else signs on their own phone: open the same scorecard link, tap SCORE THIS GROUP, tap your name, then tap Follow along. Don't tap Take over; that's only for when the scorekeeper's phone dies.

Your card opens. Check your row and tap Sign my card. If a hole is wrong, tap that number and pick the right score. The scorekeeper gets it to approve.

Please don't leave the course until your group's card is submitted and everyone has signed. That's what makes tonight's results official.

If the scorekeeper's phone dies, another player opens the link, taps their name and taps Take over. Every saved hole stays, and they pick up from the next one.

Thanks for being part of this. It's a big step for TGF, and it's ours.

See you tonight!
