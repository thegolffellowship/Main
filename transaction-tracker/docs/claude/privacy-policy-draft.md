## Privacy Policy

Effective: [date you publish]

The Golf Fellowship, LLC ("TGF", "we", "us") runs golf events and a golf
community in San Antonio and Austin, Texas. This policy explains what
information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

**Information we collect**
- Contact details: your name, email address, mobile phone number and mailing
  address.
- Golf details: your handicap index, GHIN number if you give it, scores,
  pairings, tee choices, event results and standings.
- Event and membership details: the events and games you sign up for, your
  membership, and your RSVPs.
- Payment details: what you paid and how. Card payments are handled by our
  payment processors; we do not store your full card number. If you win money,
  we keep the payout account you give us (for example your Venmo, PayPal,
  Zelle, Cash App or Apple Cash handle).
- Photos taken at our events.
- Basic website information, such as pages visited, collected through cookies.

**How we use it**
- To run our events: registration, pairings, tee times, scoring, handicaps,
  results and payouts.
- To contact you about your events, membership and account by email and, if
  you agree, by text message.
- To send you news and invitations about TGF events. You can unsubscribe at
  any time.
- To keep accurate records, including for taxes and accounting.

**What other people can see**
Names, scores, standings, results and event photos may appear on our public
leaderboards, results pages, emails and social media. We do not publish your
email address, phone number, payment details or payout accounts.

**Who we share it with**
We share information only with service providers that help us run TGF, such as
our website and online store, payment processors, email and text message
providers, and the systems that host our records. They may use it only to
provide their service to us. We may also share information when the law
requires it. **We do not sell your personal information.**

**Text messages**
If you agree to receive text messages from TGF, we use your mobile number only
to send you messages about your TGF events and account. No mobile information
will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional
purposes. Text messaging opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any
third parties. See our SMS Terms below.

**Your choices**
- Unsubscribe from marketing emails with the link in any email.
- Stop text messages at any time by replying STOP.
- Ask us to see, correct or delete your information by emailing
  [contact email]. We may need to keep some records, such as payment and tax
  records, as the law requires.

**Security**
We use reasonable measures to protect your information. No system is perfectly
secure, so please contact us if you notice anything wrong.

**Children**
Our events and website are for adults. We do not knowingly collect information
from children under 13.

**Changes**
We may update this policy. The effective date above shows when it last changed.

**Contact us**
The Golf Fellowship, LLC
[mailing address or PO box]
[contact email]

## SMS Terms

**TGF text messages.** By checking the text message box when you register or
update your profile, you agree to receive text messages from The Golf
Fellowship at the mobile number you provided. We text about your TGF events and
account: tee times and groups, changes to your group, open spots, payouts and
reminders to finish your profile.

- **Frequency:** varies, usually 2 to 8 messages a month.
- **Cost:** message and data rates may apply.
- **Stop:** reply STOP at any time. You'll get one message confirming you're
  unsubscribed and no more after that.
- **Help:** reply HELP, or email [contact email].
- **Not required:** agreeing to texts is not a condition of registering for an
  event or buying anything.
- Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
- **Privacy:** see our Privacy Policy above. No mobile information will be
  shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional
  purposes, and text messaging opt-in data and consent will not be shared with
  any third parties.
