Every TGF season ends somewhere. This past weekend it ended at The Hideout Golf Club & Resort, where twelve players from San Antonio and twelve from Austin met for the Lone Star Cup: three days of team match play, Ryder Cup style. San Antonio kept the Cup, 17½ to 10½. Here is what that weekend looks like from the inside.

Saturday night, after 36 holes.

It came down to the clock. San Antonio started Sunday needing five singles wins. Daniel S., Luke M., Jeff R. and Jeff Y. delivered four of them in half an hour. Gus V. closed out the fifth with a par on the 15th, less than two minutes after Jeff, and that was the Cup.

One eagle in 28 matches. David W. made a 3 on the par-5 9th on Sunday, the only eagle of the Cup, on his way to a big singles win for Austin.

Nobody quits out here. Matt J. was 3 down through 7 on Sunday. He shot 71, one under and the best round of the day, and won his match. Austin also won the final match on the course, so nobody went home quiet.

Players came from all over. Golfers made the trip from across Texas and as far as Louisiana for this one weekend.

How you get there

The Lone Star Cup is where the TGF season is headed. Twelve players from each city earn their seat over the year, through the points races and match play on our regular Tuesday nights and Saturday 18s. Every round you play counts toward it.

The golf was the reason, but the weekend was the dinners: Friday night at Sectionhand Steakhouse, and a long table at Pogue Farm Market after 36 holes on Saturday. People who were strangers a season ago sat down as teammates and rivals, and everyone went home tired and glad they came.

Every match and every card is on the Lone Star Cup board.
