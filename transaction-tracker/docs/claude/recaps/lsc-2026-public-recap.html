Every TGF season has one weekend everyone circles on the calendar. This past weekend it was at The Hideout Golf Club & Resort, where San Antonio and Austin met for the Lone Star Cup: three days of team match play, Ryder Cup style, fourteen players a side. San Antonio kept the Cup, 17½ to 10½. Here is what that weekend looks like from the inside.

Saturday night at Pogue Farm Market, after 36 holes.

It came down to the clock. San Antonio started Sunday needing five singles wins. Daniel S., Luke M., Jeff R. and Jeff Y. delivered four of them in half an hour. Gus V. closed out the fifth with a par on the 15th, less than two minutes after Jeff, and that was the Cup.

One eagle in 28 matches. David W. made a 3 on the par-5 9th on Sunday, the only eagle of the Cup, on his way to a big singles win for Austin.

Nobody quits out here. Matt J. was 3 down through 7 on Sunday. He shot 71, one under and the best round of the day, and won his match. Austin also won the final match on the course, so nobody went home quiet.

Players came from all over. Golfers made the trip from across Texas and as far as Louisiana for this one weekend.

How you get there

The Lone Star Cup is the celebration of the TGF season. Players earn their seat over the year, through the points races and match play on our regular Tuesday nights and Saturday 18s. It is usually twelve a side; this year there were enough to make it fourteen. Every round you play counts toward it.

Off the course, we ate together both nights: Friday at Sectionhand Steakhouse, and Saturday at that long table at Pogue Farm Market after 36 holes. People who were strangers a season ago sat down as teammates and rivals, and everyone went home tired and glad they came.

Every match and every card is on the Lone Star Cup board.
