Good Evening, %first_name%!

The Lone Star Cup stays in San Antonio. Over three sessions and 28 matches at The Hideout
Golf Club & Resort, San Antonio beat Austin 17½ to 10½ to keep the Cup.

**THE CLINCHER**

**Gus VASQUEZ** won it. In Match 22 he closed out **Kaleb MCDONNELL** 5&3 with a par on
the par-3 15th, and that point took San Antonio past 14 and put the Cup out of Austin's
reach. Gus was giving 14 strokes and never trailed: he won the first two holes, was 3 up
through 12, and finished it on 15.

It came down to minutes. San Antonio started Sunday at 9½ and needed five singles wins.
**Daniel SOUTH** got the first, 6&4 over **Chris CANNON**, and **Luke MAZANEC** the
second, 4&3 over **Luke YOUNGS**, three minutes later. **Jeff RIDEOUT** made it three with
a 6&5 win over **Kyle FRANZ**, and **Jeff YOUNG** made it four, 3&2 over Walter Hogue.
Gus closed out his match less than two minutes after Jeff.
