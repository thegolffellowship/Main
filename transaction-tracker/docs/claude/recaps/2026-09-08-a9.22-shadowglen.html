Five games were on offer Tuesday night at ShadowGlen, and Luke Youngs cashed in all five. He shot 39 / 37 net (+1) off the Bronze tees for 8 points, tied Neal Cloer for the Individual Net, split City MVP with him, took three skins (a par on 6 and back-to-back birdies on 7 and 8, a 3 on the 411-yard par 4 and a 2 on the par 3), won the closest-to-pin on 8, and rode the winning Team Net side home. A $130.88 night. It is also three fall events and three paydays for Luke: low-flight net winner at the Kickoff, second in the net at Star Ranch, and now this.

Neal got his 39 / 37 the other way around, steady through 8 and a birdie 4 on the par-5 9th to finish. Across the state, San Antonio's Jeff Rideout took TGF MVP for the night with 10 points at Silverhorn, 10 to 8, his second straight week doing it.

Last week's Austin headline, John Wade — MVP at Star Ranch with a 39 / 34 (−2) — posted 44 / 38 (+2) Tuesday and still took a skin with a par on 2. Austin Manager and two-time Players Cup Champion Robert Straiton shot 40 / 40 (+4), all gross, no strokes, and captained the winning Team Net foursome with Matthew Johnston, Luke Youngs and Jeff Sekiguchi. Joshua Bartz took the closest-to-pin on 6. Carlos Zapata's par on 1 held up for a skin.

And then there is Tom Donovan, who in his very first TGF round walked off with three skins — including one on the 4th hole with a BOGEY. That is how skins work, folks: you do not have to be good, you have to be alone. The low net of the night belonged to Lee Vasquez, 45 / 35 (−1) off the Bronze tees, with no games in play. The buyers thank you, Lee.
