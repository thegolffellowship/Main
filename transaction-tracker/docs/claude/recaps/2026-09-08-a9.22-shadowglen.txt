Five games were on offer Tuesday night at ShadowGlen (https://tgf-austin.golfgenius.com/pages/5790752?round_id=1702842), and Luke Youngs cashed in all five. He shot 39 / 37 net (+1) off the Bronze tees for 8 points, tied Neal Cloer for the Individual Net, split City MVP with him, took three skins (a par on 6 and back-to-back birdies on 7 and 8, a 3 on the 411-yard par 4 and a 2 on the par 3), won the closest-to-pin on 8, and rode the winning Team Net side home. A $130.88 night. It is also three fall events and three paydays for Luke: low-flight net winner at the Kickoff, second in the net at Star Ranch, and now this.

Neal got his 39 / 37 the other way around, steady through 8 and a birdie 4 on the par-5 9th to finish. Across the state, San Antonio's Jeff Rideout took TGF MVP for the night with 10 points at Silverhorn (https://tgf-sa.golfgenius.com/pages/5783307?round_id=1708028), 10 to 8, his second straight week doing it.

Last week's Austin headline, John Wade — MVP at Star Ranch with a 39 / 34 (−2) — posted 44 / 38 (+2) Tuesday and still took a skin with a par on 2. Austin Manager and two-time Players Cup Champion Robert Straiton shot 40 / 40 (+4), all gross, no strokes, and captained the winning Team Net foursome with Matthew Johnston, Luke Youngs and Jeff Sekiguchi. Joshua Bartz took the closest-to-pin on 6. Carlos Zapata's par on 1 held up for a skin.

And then there is Tom Donovan, who in his very first TGF round walked off with three skins — including one on the 4th hole with a BOGEY. That is how skins work, folks: you do not have to be good, you have to be alone.
