Tuesday night both of our cities were on the tee — San Antonio at Silverhorn Golf Club of Texas (RESULTS), Austin at ShadowGlen Golf Club (RESULTS). Three things happened that say more about TGF than any brochure could.

Half the field went home with money. 12 of 24 players in San Antonio and 9 of 16 players in Austin cashed something Tuesday — a skin, a closest-to-pin, a share of a team pot. Your own ball, your own handicap, your own shot at it.

First round, first payday. Christopher E. teed it up with us for the first time Tuesday night and left with money — 6 of the 8 first-timers on the sheet did. Everybody gets a fair game.

A bogey won money. On the 4th hole in Austin, Tom D. — in his first TGF round — made bogey, and it was the best score anyone posted on that hole. In our skins game every hole is its own small contest, so one good hole pays even when the rest of the round doesn't. That is how a bogey beats a birdie made two holes later.

How a TGF Event works

Connect. We handle the course, the tee times, and the pairings. You sign up, show up, and play with a group that's rooting for you.

Compete. Your own ball. A Team best ball game included, so your foursome roots for you. Optional Individual Net & Gross games. A TGF Handicap and flighting keeps it fair.

Celebrate. San Antonio went to Max & Louie's for food and fellowship. Austin grabbed drinks in the clubhouse. It's the best part, and it's yours if you want it.
