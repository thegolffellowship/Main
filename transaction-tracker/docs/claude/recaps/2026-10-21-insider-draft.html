Tuesday night both of our cities were on the tee — San Antonio at TPC San Antonio - Canyons (RESULTS), Austin at Avery Ranch Golf Club (RESULTS). Half the field in San Antonio and a third in Austin went home with money — your own ball, your own handicap, your own shot at it.

Tuesdays are the easy first step. Nine holes, late afternoon, done by dark. You enter and you're in the team game, closest-to-the-pin and the hole-in-one pot — that's every player. The individual games are an add-on you can skip any week. Two first-timers played Tuesday night and both left with money. One of them, Adam W., had the best net round of anyone in either city, in his very first TGF event.

Saturdays are the bigger day out. Eighteen holes, morning tee times, more golf and more time with your group. Sometimes it's a road trip to the other city's course. On Saturday, October 31, we're at Kissing Tree Golf Club for the Fall Championship — it's the final event of the fall points race, and it's open to everyone.

You pick the pace. Some members play every Tuesday and every Saturday. Some play once a month. Some stick to nines, some only the 18s. You sign up when it fits, and there's a spot for you either way.

How a TGF Event works

Connect. We handle the course, the tee times, and the pairings. You sign up, show up, and play with a group that's rooting for you.

Compete. Your own ball. A Team best ball game included, so your foursome roots for you. Optional Individual Net & Gross games. A TGF Handicap and flighting keeps it fair.

Celebrate. Grab a drink, stick around for good conversation. It's the best part, and it's yours if you want it.
