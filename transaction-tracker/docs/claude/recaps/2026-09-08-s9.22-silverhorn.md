**Subject:** TGF Results | RIDEOUT Sweeps the Night at Silverhorn

---

Three events into the fall and the San Antonio race already has a
shape. Tuesday night at **Silverhorn** ([RESULTS](https://tgf-sa.golfgenius.com/pages/5783307?round_id=1708028))
belonged to **Jeff Rideout**: 49 / 35 net (−1) off the Black tees was
good for 10 points, the Flight 2 net title, City MVP, and then TGF MVP
over Austin's co-MVPs Luke Youngs and Neal Cloer, 10 points to 8. Add a
share of the Team Net pot and it was a $159.50 night. Not bad for a
Tuesday.

**Gus Vasquez** took Flight 1 with a 42 / 37 (+1), and **Adam Baker**
swept both closest-to-pins, on 13 and on 16. The Team Net went to a
dead heat: **Daniel South, Hector Hinojosa, Adam Baker and Jeff
Rideout** tied **Gus Vasquez, Chuck Fehlis, Kerry Niester and Wade
Lewis**, and the pot split.

The round of the night, though, came from a player who did not buy a
single game. **Daniel South** posted 38 / 34 net (−2), the low net in
the field, with birdies on 10, 11, 17 and 18 the way his card reads.
That is a lot of golf to leave on the table. The games are right here
when you check out: [thegolffellowship.com/shop/ols/products/s9-23-the-quarry](https://thegolffellowship.com/shop/ols/products/s9-23-the-quarry).

**Pat Youngs** shot the number: 36 on the nose, even par, while giving
strokes back as a plus handicap — all gross, no help. Three skins came
with it (birdie on 14, then pars on 15 and 16 that nobody could match).
Larry Anthis, Kerry Niester, Jeff Young and first-timer Christopher
Espinosa also took skins home, which means a par on 10 in your first
TGF round bought Christopher a check. Half the field cashed something
Tuesday — twelve different players went home with money.
