Jeff Rideout shot 49 / 35 net (−1) off the Black tees for 10 points, won Flight 2, took City MVP, and then took TGF MVP over Austin's co-MVPs Luke Youngs and Neal Cloer (8 points each at [ShadowGlen](https://tgf-austin.golfgenius.com/pages/5790752?round_id=1702842)), 10 to 8. Gus Vasquez took Flight 1 with a 42 / 37 (+1). Adam Baker swept both closest-to-pins, on 13 and on 16, a week after finishing second in the net — he has cashed both Tuesdays of the fall. The Team Net went to a dead heat at 30 (-6): Daniel South, Hector Hinojosa, Adam Baker and Jeff Rideout tied Gus Vasquez, Chuck Fehlis, Kerry Niester and Wade Lewis, and the pot split.

Then there is Daniel South, who leads the Fall Points Race, posted the low net in the field Tuesday at 38 / 34 (−2) with a birdie on the par-5 18th and three more net birdies. He would be taking all our money if he bought into games, so thank you, Dan!

Pat Youngs shot the number: 36 on the nose, even par, while giving strokes back as a plus handicap — all gross, no help. Three skins came with it (a birdie on 14, then pars on 15 and 16 that nobody could match). Larry Anthis, Kerry Niester, Jeff Young and first-timer Christopher Espinosa also took skins home, which means a par on 10 in your first TGF round bought Christopher some cash.

Half the field cashed something Tuesday — twelve different players went home with money.
