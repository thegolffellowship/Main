# Live Score Entry: Manager Runbook (one page)

For the chapter manager running an event on the Tracker's score entry
(CA #829, for events from 10/13). Golf Genius stays the official record
until Kerry says otherwise. Score entry never takes payments and never
emails anyone.

## The day before
1. **Save the PAIRINGS** on the event (EVENTS → the event → PAIRINGS →
   Generate or edit → Save). Nothing below works without saved pairings.
2. **Kerry (admin) seeds score entry** from the SCORE ENTRY panel under
   the Pairings toolbar: **Seed 9-hole score entry** (or 18). One scoring
   group per foursome appears, each with its players. Seeding is admin-only
   for now; ask Kerry if it hasn't been done.
   - Changed the pairings after seeding? Tap **Update N-hole groups from
     pairings**. Groups and handicaps update, and scores are kept.
3. Check that the panel shows **OPEN** and that every group has its players.
   The chips show two switches: **Members** and **Cart-sign QR**. Kerry
   turns those on; don't expect them to be on until he does.

## Getting each group its link
- **Cart signs:** with the Cart-sign QR switch on, each printed cart sign
  carries its group's QR in the corner ("Scan to keep score").
- **From the panel:** **QR** shows a group's code on your screen to scan.
  **Copy link** copies it to text to a player.
- One person in each group keeps score: they open the link, tap **their
  own name**, and see "You're keeping score". Everyone else who opens the
  link sees the card fill in live and signs at the end.

## During the round
- **Weak signal is fine.** Scores wait on the phone and send themselves.
  An amber "N holes waiting to send" means it's still trying. Keep
  playing, and don't clear the browser or close the tab in the middle of
  a hole.
- **Closest to the pin / Longest putt:** after the scorekeeper saves a
  CTP hole, the phone asks who in the group is closest. It only asks on
  the holes the games matrix plays for the field size. No measuring;
  the next group sees the current holder.
- **Something's wrong:** a player taps the wrong number on their own row
  and the scorekeeper gets the flag to fix it.
- **Hole-in-one:** a 1 on a par 3 or 4 opens a claim. The scorekeeper
  confirms, one other player in the group confirms, then it waits for
  verification: **Verify** on the Live Scoring page (see "Manager actions").

## When a phone dies (or the scorekeeper changes)
1. On any other phone in the group, open the same link (QR or text).
2. Tap **Take over scoring**. The card moves to that phone. There is no
   timer: two phones can never both be keeping the card.
3. Check the last few holes on the card against the paper card. A hole
   the dead phone never sent is simply blank: enter it again. If the dead
   phone comes back, its old scores are refused and kept on record, never
   applied over the new phone's.

## Finishing
1. After the last hole the scorekeeper sees **Every hole is in** →
   **Check the card** against the paper card → a photo of the paper
   card → **Submit**.
2. Each player signs their own card on their phone. If the event's
   "scorekeeper signs for the group" setting is on, Submit signs for
   everyone, and a player can still flag a hole afterwards.
3. When the panel shows every group finished, tap **Close round**. The
   links stop opening. Nothing is deleted, and the scores are kept.

## If score entry isn't working at all
Keep the paper cards. Golf Genius is still the record, so the round is
scored as it always was. Tell Kerry which group and what happened.

## Manager actions (the Live Scoring page)
Open the event → PAIRINGS → **Live Scoring**. Under each round, **Manager
actions** has the four things only a manager does. Each one is logged
with who did it, and the last few show at the bottom of the panel.
- **CTP / Longest putt:** pick the winner and tap **Settle** to decide a
  disagreement. It replaces the current holder for every group.
- **Hole-in-one:** once the scorekeeper and one other player have
  confirmed, tap **Verify** (or **Reject**). Nothing is announced to
  members from here.
- **Flags:** a player flagged a hole. Check with the group, then tap
  **Clear the flag** and say how it was resolved.
- **Sign for a player:** a complete card that wasn't signed (a player
  left early). A note is required. A flagged card can't be signed until
  its flag is cleared.

## Needs Kerry
- Seeding score entry for the event.
- Turning on the **Members** or **Cart-sign QR** switches.

## Don'ts
- Don't change the pairings after tee time and re-seed mid-round unless
  Kerry asks. It's safe for scores, but groups can move.
- No changes to the Tracker go live during the round: the no-push window
  runs from first tee to the posted close-out (CA #829).
