# What happened in 2022–2025 — Kerry's account + the archive numbers (2026-09-17)

Kerry, the Insider Writer interview, 2026-09-17 evening, verbatim:

> "I thought that I'd try to split the chapters into geographic regions and
> allow people to buy into each one as a separate points race. I had also
> run a survey about what members thought the value of a TGF Membership
> was. I landed on $200 based on that survey, and offered different levels
> based on what season contests they wanted, basically bundling it all
> together. I don't know that it totally backfired but timing was horrible
> because there was a return to work from COVID that happened, regular
> inflation and golf supply and demand totally flipping causing a double
> inflation in the golf world. I lost a lot of younger members who didn't
> have as much control over their time or as much discretionary money to
> handle the deferred increases in costs we were getting from the courses.
> I lost other members who were asked to run larger territories or do more
> with less because COVID was such a disruptor to the workforce. It was a
> nightmare in 23 and 24. Then we made a rebound in 25 like you said and
> it's felt more holding steady in 26… but as I've mentioned before I've
> done very little paid adverts this year while I've been building the app."

## What it means (tracker-claude's reading, for Kerry to correct)

1. **2023 changed three things at once, on top of a macro shock:** the
   geographic split (half-fields by design), a $200 bundled membership
   with contest tiers, and a founder newly full-time — while golf demand
   and course costs both jumped and workplaces called people back. The
   data cannot separate those causes; Kerry's account says the members
   he lost were TIME-constrained and MONEY-constrained, not bored.
2. **The 2024 simplification IS the answer to 2023.** Per-event pay,
   a low annual fee, no dues, "Try a Tuesday". The 2025 rebuild proves
   the product; Austin went from 23 people to 149.
3. **2026 "holding steady" with almost no paid acquisition is the
   notable fact.** The base holds itself. So 2027's growth lever is
   reach (paid acquisition back on, the Insider as organic reach) and
   frequency (the fall drop-off), not another model change. Any 2027
   pricing/model change should be tested against the 2023 lesson:
   never raise commitment and price in the same season.
4. **For the public voice:** the twenty-seasons angle now has a true arc
   (56 a night in 2019 on ten Tuesdays; the rebuild of 2025; more golf
   than ever), but "nightmare", the split, the $200 tier and any member
   count never appear member-facing (event-recaps.md rule 1).

**Corrected reading:** 2022 did not lose its members to 2023. It lost its
Tuesday CROWD between August and September 2022, when the first fall series
ran at a third of the spring field, and the 2023 split then halved what any
one night could show. The 2023 model change coincided with a slide that had
already begun; the members Kerry describes losing (time- and money-squeezed)
are the fall-2022 no-shows as much as the 2023 non-renewals. The pattern is
the SAME one 2026 shows: a spring peak and a second-half fade — which makes
the fall drop-off the oldest problem in the business, not a new one.

## Why September empties the field — Kerry (2026-09-17, interview Q8)

Asked which of daylight, football, school schedules, hunting season, course
fall rates or something else explains the fall fade every year: *"All of
those things you mention except fall rates, plus I think because we have a
lot of business professionals we get late quarter business expectations
that demand more of them. I've always known Fall is lighter and I've not
always done Fall because of it. It's just been that I've felt there were
enough that still benefitted from it to keep it going."*

Rulings that follow for the Insider and the fall re-engagement:
- Fall is a DIFFERENT product from spring: shorter days, competing seasons,
  Q4 work pressure. Sell it as "one night when you can", never as "the race
  you're behind in". The frequency pitch ("your seventh Tuesday") is a
  spring/summer pitch; in fall it becomes "one more before the year is out".
- Fall's job is warmth into the spring kickoff (renewals, the March field),
  not volume. Measure fall by who comes back in March, not by the Tuesday
  count. The scorecard Routine reports fall fields against FALL baselines
  (2022 Sep–Nov SA 18–20; 2023 fall 13–20), never against spring.
- Kerry kept fall going for the members who still benefit — the Insider
  can say that plainly (belonging angle): the fall is for the people who
  need a Tuesday most.

## 2027 — Kerry's targets and the winter cadence (2026-09-17, interview Q9)

Verbatim: *"I'd say every other week or possibly every third week or monthly.
We will have monthly 18s and possibly every 3 week 18s during the winter
months to kind of keep the light on for people. We definitely need to be
strategic how to build towards 2027 full kickoff after spring break and to
ramp up membership. Starting off at 40 S.A. and 30 Austin averages for
Tuesdays would be a realistically achievable goal. That would be about 50%
of current membership numbers. If we could gain 20–40 new members (perhaps
a net gain of 15–30 with turnover), which I think is realistic, we could hit
those numbers. That's going to require more lead campaign work. People are
always moving to San Antonio. We want to catch them first if possible. So
there's always an aspect of getting our name out there in front of people
that have never heard of us. And there are tens of thousands of golfers in
SA and Austin. We just need our share of them who really yearn for the value
we provide."*

## The rate lever — what has been tried (Kerry, 2026-09-17, interview Q10)

Verbatim: *"I don't think I really have tried anything specifically other
than personal text in regards to moving a member from one Tuesday in three
to one in two. We already have playing partner request and I don't think
that standing tee time or preferences work for that. I could see
game-finding things where there's benefits from playing events in a row
like discounts or credits but we've also talked about the TGF Plus
membership which basically you pay more upfront, but you pay less, basically
at cost, for each event so the benefit comes when you play more. So there's
kind of a built-in incentive that if I'm gonna sign up for the Plus, then I
need to play more to get my value out of it. I'm thinking once we get that
going for 2027 that may be the single best kind of backdoor way of
incentivizing more play and benefiting those who really deserve it or die
hard… but I do believe that a captain texting his group Monday night — or
before, really not a captain necessarily but like an ambassador or a crew
leader or a team leader or something — of like 12 members, texting and
keeping in touch with them could be a motivational, kind of peer effort and
FOMO that could potentially work. I'm open to other ideas, but I have a
feeling that those are kind of our primary options."*

Standing facts: only personal texts from Kerry have ever been tried; the
playing-partner request exists; standing tee times ruled out; Plus
membership is the 2027 structural incentive (never named in public copy —
banned word); crews of ~12 with a leader texting before Tuesday is the
peer-pressure option. Proposal and pilot design: `docs/claude/crews.md`
(pending Kerry's pick of the first two crew leaders).
